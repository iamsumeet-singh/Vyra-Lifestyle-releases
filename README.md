# Vyra for Android

Download the latest APK from the **[Releases page](../../releases/latest)** and install it directly on your phone. No Play Store account is needed.

**Latest: v1.5** · [Download Vyra-v1.5.apk](../../releases/latest/download/Vyra-v1.5.apk)

## Requirements

- Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer
- About 10 MB to download

## How to install

1. On your phone, open the link above and download `Vyra-v1.5.apk`.
2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager (**Install unknown apps**). Turn that on for that app only.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

If your phone says "Play Protect blocked this app", tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. The app is signed by its developer but isn't listed on the Play Store, so Play Protect doesn't recognise it.

## Updating

Install a newer APK over the old one. Your data stays, because every release is signed with the same key.

Some features (Gmail scanning, Google Drive backup, AI features) only work after you sign in or add your own API key inside the app. Anything you enter is stored on your device.
